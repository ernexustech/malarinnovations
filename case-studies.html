Case studies

Implementation records, not brochures.

Representative engagement summaries. Client names withheld on request; detailed references available on qualified enquiries.

CASE 01 — ISO 9001

QMS implementation, precision components manufacturer

Challenge: Recurring customer complaints and inconsistent process documentation across two plants.

Approach: Gap assessment, process mapping, document control system, internal auditor training for 12 staff.

Result: Certification achieved with zero major non-conformities; customer complaint rate reduced within two quarters.

CASE 02 — ISO 50001

Energy management system, textile processing unit

Challenge: Rising energy costs with no structured measurement or accountability.

Approach: Energy baseline, metering plan, ISO 50001 EnMS design, operator training.

Result: Verified energy performance improvement and a certified EnMS supporting ongoing savings tracking.

CASE 03 — LEAN TRANSFORMATION

5S, VSM and Kaizen rollout, discrete manufacturer

Challenge: Long changeover times and high work-in-progress inventory.

Approach: Value stream mapping, SMED workshops, 5S implementation, visual management boards.

Result: Reduced changeover time and WIP, with a trained internal Kaizen team to sustain gains.

CASE 04 — ENERGY AUDIT

Detailed energy audit, mid-size manufacturing facility

Challenge: No visibility into where energy was being lost across utilities and process lines.

Approach: Metering, thermography survey, load analysis, savings opportunity register.

Result: Prioritized savings roadmap with payback periods, several no-cost measures implemented immediately.

CASE 05 — ESG / BRSR

First-year ESG and BRSR reporting, listed manufacturer

Challenge: New regulatory obligation with no internal ESG data process.

Approach: Materiality assessment, data collection framework, BRSR-aligned disclosure drafting, team training.

Result: Filed on schedule with a repeatable internal process for subsequent reporting cycles.

CASE 06 — ISO 17025

Laboratory accreditation readiness, testing laboratory

Challenge: Laboratory processes not yet structured for accreditation body assessment.

Approach: Gap assessment against ISO/IEC 17025, method validation review, quality manual development, mock assessment.

Result: Laboratory submitted for accreditation with documented technical competence and quality processes.
