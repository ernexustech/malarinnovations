Frequently asked

Before you book a consultation.

How long does ISO certification typically take?＋

Most organizations move from gap assessment to certification audit in 3–6 months, depending on management system maturity, site count and the standard involved.

Do you provide training for internal teams, not just external audits?＋

Yes. Our Training Academy runs Internal Auditor, Lean Six Sigma and sustainability programs designed to build in-house capability so you're not dependent on consultants after certification.

Can you support multi-site or multi-standard integrated audits?＋

Yes. We regularly design integrated management systems covering ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 together, and coordinate multi-site rollouts on a common audit calendar.

Do you work with certification bodies directly?＋

We prepare you for the certification body of your choice and can liaise on scheduling and scope, while remaining independent of the certification decision itself.
