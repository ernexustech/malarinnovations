About the practice

Knowledge, standards and practical implementation.

Malar Innovation & Automation Solutions is a professional consulting, training, auditing, engineering and sustainability advisory organization dedicated to helping businesses improve performance, compliance, operational excellence and long-term sustainability.

COMPANY OVERVIEW

Who we are

We partner with organizations to implement internationally recognized standards, strengthen governance systems, enhance operational efficiency, reduce business risk, improve energy performance, and support sustainable growth. Our work spans certification-ready management systems, internal auditor development, electrical engineering studies, industrial automation and ESG disclosure.

HOW WE WORK

Implementation, not paperwork

Every engagement starts with a gap assessment against the relevant standard, followed by procedure design, workforce training, internal audit cycles, and certification body liaison — so the system is genuinely operated by your people, not maintained by a consultant on retainer.
